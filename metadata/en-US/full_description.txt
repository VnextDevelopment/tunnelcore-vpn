TunnelCore VPN is a VPN app for Android with flexible server selection and clear connection controls. Connect through an available VPN server and choose how apps use your connection.

EASY VPN CONNECTION
Sign in, select an available server and connect in a few taps. View your connection status directly in the app.

AUTOMATIC OR MANUAL SERVER SELECTION
Let TunnelCore VPN choose an available server automatically, or pick a location yourself. Available servers and locations depend on your account and service availability.

SPLIT TUNNELING
On supported Android phones and tablets, choose which apps use the VPN and which connect directly to the internet. Split tunneling is not offered in the Android TV interface.

SIGN IN YOUR WAY
Use a PIN code from the TunnelCore Telegram bot, sign in with your email and password, or create an account. Email users can link their Telegram account using a PIN.

24-HOUR TRIAL
Eligible new users can activate a one-time 24-hour trial. An active paid subscription is required to keep using the TunnelCore VPN service after the trial.

TunnelCore VPN uses a VPN connection to encrypt traffic between your device and the selected VPN server. Connection quality and server availability may vary by network and location.
