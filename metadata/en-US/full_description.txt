TunnelCore VPN is a paid VPN service for Android with a one-time 24-hour trial for eligible new users. Connect to an available VPN server and manage your connection with a simple, user-friendly interface.

EASY VPN CONNECTION
Sign in, select an available VPN server and connect in a few taps. View your VPN connection status and disconnect whenever you choose.

AUTOMATIC OR MANUAL SERVER SELECTION
Let TunnelCore VPN select an available server automatically, or manually choose from available server locations. Server and location availability depends on your account and network conditions.

SPLIT TUNNELING
On supported Android phones and tablets, choose which apps use the VPN connection and which connect directly to the internet. Split tunneling is not available in the Android TV interface.

SIGN IN WITH PIN OR EMAIL
• Sign in with a PIN code obtained from the TunnelCore Telegram bot.
• Sign in with your email address and password.
• Create an account with email and password.
• Link an email account to Telegram with a PIN code.

24-HOUR TRIAL AND SUBSCRIPTION
Eligible new users can activate a one-time 24-hour VPN trial. After the trial, an active paid subscription is required to continue using the TunnelCore VPN service.

VPN SERVICE AND ENCRYPTION
TunnelCore VPN uses the Android VpnService API exclusively to establish and manage VPN connections. It creates a network tunnel between your Android device and the selected TunnelCore VPN server using the AmneziaWG protocol. Network traffic routed through that tunnel is encrypted between your device and the VPN server.

The VpnService API is not used to manipulate or redirect traffic from other apps for advertising monetization. You remain in control of your VPN connection and can connect or disconnect at any time.
